    User guide

    Equipment

    • A printed, laminated Cup of Carbon background sheet, or one in a clear plastic sleeve.
    • A straight-sided white mug, approximately 300 mL, with an internal depth of 7–9 cm.
    • A flat surface, such as a clipboard.
    • A smartphone or digital camera.
    • A Cup of Carbon account.

    Create an account

    1. Open the Cup of Carbon web app and choose Sign In, then Sign Up Now.
    2. Enter a username (2–6 characters), email address, and password. The password must be at least eight characters and contain an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special character. Enter it again to confirm.
    3. Complete the reCAPTCHA and select Sign Up.
    4. After the account is created, sign in with your email address and password.

    The registration page says that your email address is used by the Cup of Carbon development team for important app announcements or password-reset assistance, and is not shared beyond the University of Glasgow.

    Photograph a sample

    1. Fill the white mug with the water sample.
    2. Place the mug on the background sheet, aligning its handle with the guide marks.
    3. Keep the setup in even shade. Avoid direct sunlight, glare, and reflections.
    4. Photograph from approximately 30–40 cm above the mug. Include the entire background sheet in the image.
    5. Sign in to the Cup of Carbon web app, choose Upload, submit the photograph, and provide the sampling location or notes if prompted.

    Improve your results

    • Take three to five replicate photographs and average the results.
    • The method performs best for samples in the 0–35 mg/L DOC range.
    • Dilute very dark samples with colourless water before analysis.
    • Filter samples with substantial suspended sediment or peat: this method estimates dissolved, not particulate, organic carbon.
